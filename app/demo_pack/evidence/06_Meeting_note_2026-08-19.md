**Item 4 — East Africa surveillance agreements (OBJ-2026-07)**

Joseph reported Kenya signed on 30 June, covering two of the five priority disease categories. The remaining three would need to be pursued through the regional protocol, which is a separate process on an unconfirmed timetable.

Ethiopia remains stalled pending a data-protection annex. The Partnerships and Legal Office confirmed that no drafter is assigned and that the DPO review has not been requested.

The Finance Business Partner confirmed that the budget line for the **Tanzania scoping mission, previously held for early September, was reallocated in the July reforecast** to cover the extension of the Kenya field team. No replacement provision has been made in this financial year. The Tanzania engagement therefore cannot open before January at the earliest.

MELIA asked whether the objective should be re-baselined, given that the success measure specifies three signed agreements covering five disease categories each, and that on the current trajectory the year will close with one agreement covering two categories.

**Agreed:** Amina to take a view on re-baselining before the Q3 submission window closes. No decision taken at this meeting.
